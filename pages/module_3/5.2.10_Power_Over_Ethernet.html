5.2.10 Power over Ethernet

What is PoE?

Power over Ethernet (PoE) supplies electrical power from a switch port over ordinary data cabling to a powered device (PD) such as a VoIP handset, IP camera, or wireless access point — eliminating the need for a separate AC adapter at each device.

PoE Standards

Standard Name Usable Power Max Current Typical Devices
802.3af PoE / Type 1 / 2-pair PoE ~13 W 350 mA @ 48V (15.4W supplied, ~13W usable over 100m) VoIP phones, basic APs, basic security cameras
802.3at PoE+ / Type 2 ~25 W 600 mA Advanced APs, pan-tilt-zoom cameras, video IP phones
802.3bt Type 3 PoE++ / 4PPoE ~51 W — LED lighting, digital signage, point-of-sale systems
802.3bt Type 4 PoE++ / 4PPoE ~73 W — High-power devices

PoE Switch (Endspan PSE)

A PoE-enabled switch is called endspan power sourcing equipment (PSE). When a device is connected, the switch performs a detection phase to determine if the device is PoE-enabled, then sets an appropriate supply voltage. Non-PoE devices are not damaged — no power is supplied if PoE is not detected.

Benefits over individual AC adapters: more efficient, enables network management software to control devices, apply sleep states, and power cap unused devices.

PoE Injector (Midspan)

If the switch does not support PoE, a power injector (midspan) can be used inline: one port connects to the switch, the other connects to the powered device. Total cable length still cannot exceed 100m.

Key Terms

PoE (Power over Ethernet)
A technology that delivers electrical power over standard Ethernet data cabling to powered devices, removing the need for separate power supplies.
Powered device (PD)
Any PoE-compatible device that receives power over the Ethernet cable, such as a VoIP phone, IP camera, or wireless access point.
Endspan PSE (Power Sourcing Equipment)
A PoE-capable switch that supplies power directly from its ports. Performs device detection before supplying power to avoid damaging non-PoE devices.
PoE injector (midspan)
An inline device that adds PoE capability between a non-PoE switch and a powered device. Total cable run must remain within 100m.
