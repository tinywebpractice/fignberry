5.3.10 Copper Cabling Installation Considerations

Plenum Cable

A plenum space is a void used for HVAC airflow, typically a false ceiling or raised floor. Because airflow makes plenum spaces an effective conduit for fire, building regulations require fire-retardant plenum-rated cable in these areas.

Cable Type Jacket Material NEC Marking Notes
General purpose PVC CMG or CM Standard indoor cable; not fire-retardant enough for plenum spaces.
Plenum-rated Treated PVC or FEP (fluorinated ethylene polymer) CMP Must not emit large amounts of smoke when burned and must be self-extinguishing. Less flexible than PVC but bandwidth is unaffected.

Outside Plant (OSP) Cable

OSP cable runs on external building walls or between buildings. Regular PVC cable is not suitable for outdoor use. OSP cable uses special UV-resistant, abrasion-resistant coatings and is often gel-filled to handle temperature extremes and moisture.

OSP Type Description Special Requirements
Aerial cable Strung between poles or anchors above ground UV-resistant jacket; rated for temperature and moisture exposure.
Conduit burial Cable run inside conduit underground Conduit adds protection but cable must still be rated for temperature and moisture.
Direct burial Cable laid directly in earth or encased in concrete Gel-filled; may require armoring to protect against rodent damage.

Key Terms

Plenum space
A building void used for HVAC airflow, typically a false ceiling or raised floor. Fire regulations require CMP-rated cable in these areas.
CMP (Communications Multipurpose Plenum)
The NEC marking for plenum-rated cable. Must be self-extinguishing and produce minimal smoke when burned.
FEP (Fluorinated Ethylene Polymer)
A fire-retardant material used in plenum-rated cable jackets as an alternative to treated PVC.
OSP (Outside Plant)
Cable installed on exterior building surfaces or between buildings. Requires UV-resistant, gel-filled, or armored construction depending on installation type.
Direct burial cable
OSP cable designed to be laid directly in earth or concrete. Gel-filled for moisture/temperature protection; may be armored against rodents.
