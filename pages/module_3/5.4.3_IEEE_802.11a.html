5.4.3 IEEE 802.11a

802.11a Overview

  • Band: 5 GHz only.
  • Max data rate: 54 Mbps.
  • Channels: 23 non-overlapping channels, each 20 MHz wide.
  • Regulatory impacts: channel availability varies by country; power output is regulated, which limits range.
  • DFS requirement: devices operating in the 5 GHz band must implement Dynamic Frequency Selection (DFS) to avoid interfering with radar and satellite installations. Channels in the DFS range are disabled if radar signals are detected.

U-NII Sub-Bands (5 GHz Channel Layout)

The 5 GHz band is organized into U-NII (Unlicensed National Information Infrastructure) sub-bands. Each sub-band is 5 MHz wide; channels are spaced in intervals of four to provide 20 MHz bandwidth per channel.

Sub-band Channels DFS Required
U-NII-1 36, 40, 44, 48 No
U-NII-2 52, 56, 60, 64 Yes
U-NII-2 Extended 100, 104, 108, 112, 116, 120, 124, 128, 132, 136, 140 Yes
U-NII-3 149, 153, 157, 161 No

Key Terms

IEEE 802.11a
A Wi-Fi standard using the 5 GHz band with a maximum data rate of 54 Mbps and 23 non-overlapping 20 MHz channels.
DFS (Dynamic Frequency Selection)
A requirement for 5 GHz Wi-Fi devices to detect radar signals and automatically switch away from channels that would interfere with radar or satellite installations.
U-NII (Unlicensed National Information Infrastructure)
The sub-band groupings that make up the 5 GHz Wi-Fi band. Divided into U-NII-1, U-NII-2, U-NII-2 Extended, and U-NII-3.
