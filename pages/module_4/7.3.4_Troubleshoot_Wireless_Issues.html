Lab Scenario

A help desk ticket reports that a laptop in Office 2 cannot connect to Wi-Fi. You will verify that the wireless network is working from another computer, troubleshoot the laptop hardware, reconnect it to CorpNet, and close the ticket with a professional note.

Step 1 - Open Issue Trax

  • From the taskbar, select Start.
  • Select All apps.
  • Select Issue Trax.
  • Maximize the window for better viewing.
  • Select and read ticket #25.

Step 2 - Test Wireless from ITAdmin

  • In the Notification Area of the ITAdmin computer, select the network icon.
  • Select the arrow next to the wireless icon to view available networks.
  • Select the CorpNet wireless network.
  • Select Connect.
  • Because ITAdmin connects to CorpNet, the wireless access point is powered on and broadcasting. The problem is likely limited to the laptop in Office 2.

Step 3 - Troubleshoot Exec-Laptop

  • From the top left, select Floor 1 Overview.
  • Under Office 2, select Office2-Lap.
  • In the Notification Area, select the globe icon.
  • Notice that there is no option to connect to Wi-Fi.
  • Since ITAdmin connected to CorpNet, the likely issue is the laptop wireless network interface card being turned off.
  • From the top left, select Office 2 to switch to the hardware view.
  • On the front of the laptop, check the wireless network interface card switch.
  • Notice that the switch is in the Off position.
  • Slide the wireless switch to the On position.

Step 4 - Connect Exec-Laptop to CorpNet

  • Select the laptop monitor to view the operating system.
  • In the Notification Area, select the wireless network icon.
  • Select the arrow next to the wireless icon to view available networks.
  • Confirm that CorpNet is now displayed in the list.
  • Select the CorpNet wireless network.
  • Select Connect.
  • Because the laptop was previously connected to CorpNet, it reconnects without needing the security key.

Step 5 - Resolve the Ticket

  • Go back to the ITAdmin computer in the IT Administration Office.
  • In the New Comment field, type a polite and professional comment explaining what you did.
  • Click the plus icon to add the comment.
  • At the top of the screen, set the ticket to Closed.

Troubleshooting Summary

Observation Meaning Action
ITAdmin connects to CorpNet The wireless network is available and broadcasting. Focus troubleshooting on Office2-Lap.
Office2-Lap shows no Wi-Fi option The laptop wireless NIC may be disabled. Turn on the physical wireless switch.
CorpNet appears after turning on the switch The wireless adapter is active again. Connect to CorpNet and close ticket #25.

Important: Since another computer can connect to CorpNet, the access point and SSID broadcast are working. Troubleshoot the affected laptop next.
