10.1.12 Activity: Security Print and Badging

Activity Overview

CompTIA exercise: select the two common authentication methods that mitigate the risk of confidential printed documents being intercepted from the output tray.

Scenario

A user needs to print and collect confidential documents. The workflow shows a user sending a job to a device with a keypad, then authenticating to release the job (Authorized state). The question asks which two authentication methods protect against interception.

Answer

Correct selections: Badging and PIN Entry.

Both are secured print authentication methods that hold the job on the device until the user physically authenticates at the printer, preventing unattended documents in the output tray.

Incorrect options: "Confidential" Cover Page, Locked Room, and Security Cameras do not prevent interception of a print job at the device - they are physical or labeling controls, not print release mechanisms.
