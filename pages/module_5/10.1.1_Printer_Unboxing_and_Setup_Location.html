10.1.1 Printer Unboxing and Setup Location

Key Terminology

Printer object / Logical printer
Software representation of a printer (the driver/queue in the OS).
Print device / Physical printer
The actual hardware that produces output.

Common vendors: HP, Epson, Canon, Xerox, Brother, OKI, Konica/Minolta, Lexmark, Ricoh, Samsung.

Selecting a Printer

Criterion Details
Speed Measured in pages per minute (ppm). Monochrome text prints faster than color photos.
Resolution Measured in dots per inch (dpi). Higher dpi = better quality. Vertical and horizontal may differ (e.g., 2400x600 dpi).
Paper Handling Supported paper types/sizes (labels, envelopes, card stock). Tray capacity affects jam risk.
Options Automatic duplex unit (double-sided printing); finishing units for folding, stapling, hole punching.

Setup Location

  • Power and Network: Requires power outlet and network data port. Manage cables to avoid trip hazards.
  • Environment: Stable, flat surface; away from direct sunlight. Good ventilation to disperse fumes. Store consumables in a dry, temperature-controlled area.
  • Accessibility: Easily reachable without being disruptive. Use an access-controlled area for confidential printing.

Unboxing Checklist

  • Lifting: Many printers are heavy -- use two people. Bend at the knees, grip designated handles, clear the path of trip hazards.
  • Packing Materials: Remove all packing strips and supports before powering on. Check panels for concealed strips on removable components.
  • Acclimation: If moved from cold to warm environment, leave powered off for a few hours to prevent condensation. Store paper for a day or more to adjust to local temperature and humidity.
