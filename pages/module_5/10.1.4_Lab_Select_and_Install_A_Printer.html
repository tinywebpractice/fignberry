10.1.4 Lab: Select and Install a Printer

Required Actions

  • Add the Printer, Laser, USB printer to the workspace.
  • Connect the printer to the computer with a USB Cable A/B.
  • Provide power to the printer with an AC Power Cable.

Visual Workflow

  1. Under Inventory, expand Peripherals, then drag Printer, Laser, USB to the workspace.
  2. Switch to the back view of the printer and the back view of the computer tower.
  3. Under Inventory, select < Peripherals, then select Cables.
  4. Select USB Cable A/B. In the selected item pane, drag the Type A connector to an open USB port on the back of the computer.
  5. Drag the Type B connector to the Type B USB port on the back of the printer.
  6. Select AC Power Cable. Drag the male connector to the wall outlet.
  7. Drag the female connector to the power port on the back of the printer.

Step 1 - Add the Laser Printer

  1. Press Alt-I to access the Inventory menu.
  2. Tab to Peripherals and press Enter.
  3. Tab to Printer, Laser, USB and press Enter.
  4. Press Enter again to add the printer to the workbench.
  5. On the Select Location to Add Item window, use the connector drop-down menu to select Printer, Laser, USB.
  6. Press Tab to access the Destination drop-down menu, select Bench, then press Enter.
  7. Press Tab twice, then press Enter to confirm.

Step 2 - Connect the Printer to the Computer

  1. Press Alt-I, tab to Peripherals, and press Enter to return to the main Inventory window.
  2. Press Alt-I again, tab to Cables, and press Enter.
  3. Tab to USB Cable (A/B), press Enter, then press Enter again to add the cable.
  4. In the connector drop-down menu, select USB Type B Connector.
  5. Press Tab to access the Destination drop-down menu, select USB Port Printer, Laser, USB, then press Enter.
  6. Press Tab twice, then press Enter to confirm.
  7. Press Alt-W to access the Workspace.
  8. Tab until the arrow next to Printer, Laser, USB is highlighted, then press Enter.
  9. Press Tab twice so the arrow next to USB Type B Connector is highlighted, then press Enter.
  10. Press Tab once so Connector, USB, Type A, Standard, Thick Cable is highlighted, then press Enter twice to add the connector.
  11. In the Destination field, select Usb1, Motherboard, Socket AM3, NIC, then press Enter.
  12. Press Tab twice, then press Enter to confirm.

Step 3 - Provide Power to the Printer

  1. Press Alt-I, tab to Cables, and press Enter.
  2. Tab to AC Power Cable, press Enter, then press Enter again to add the cable.
  3. In the connector drop-down menu, select AC Power Connector (Female).
  4. Press Tab to access the Destination drop-down menu, select AC Power Printer, Laser, USB, then press Enter.
  5. Press Tab twice, then press Enter to confirm.
  6. Press Alt-W to access the Workspace.
  7. Tab until the arrow next to Printer, Laser, USB is highlighted, then press Enter.
  8. Press Tab twice so the arrow next to AC Power Connector (Female) is highlighted, then press Enter.
  9. Press Tab twice so Connector, Power, AC, Male is highlighted, then press Enter twice to add the connector.
  10. In the Destination field, select AC2 Outlet, AC 115 volt, RJ45 and RJ-11, F-type, then press Enter.
  11. Press Tab twice, then press Enter to confirm.
