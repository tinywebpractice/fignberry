10.1.7 Printing Preferences

Overview

The Printing Preferences dialog sets default print job options. Settings apply to all jobs by default but can be overridden per job via the Properties button in the application's Print dialog. Some printers also include vendor management software for adjusting these settings.

Printing Preferences Tabs

Tab Key Settings
Printing Shortcuts Saved preset templates (e.g., Factory Defaults, General Everyday Printing, Envelopes, Cardstock/Heavy, Labels, EcoSMART Settings). Select a shortcut to apply a full group of settings at once.
Paper/Quality Paper size, paper source, paper type, special pages (covers, different paper per page), EconoMode (reduced ink/toner), gloss level.
Color Switch between color and grayscale printing.
Finishing Duplex (print on both sides), multiple images per sheet, portrait or landscape orientation.
Effects Scaling and watermark options.
Advanced Additional driver-level output options.
Services Vendor-provided maintenance and diagnostic tools.
