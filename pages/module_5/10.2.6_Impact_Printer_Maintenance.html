10.2.6 Impact Printer Maintenance

Overview

Impact printers strike an inked ribbon against paper to create marks. The most common type is the dot matrix printer, which uses a column of pins in the print head to strike the ribbon. Impact printers are still used for specialized tasks such as invoices, pay slips, and multipart forms.

Multipart paper
Multiple layers with carbon or carbonless coating between them. Allows duplicate or triplicate copies in a single pass. Typically used with continuous, tractor-fed paper.

Paper Types

Paper Type How It Works Notes
Plain paper Held against the platen, or moving roller, and pulled through by friction as the platen rotates. Optional cut sheet feeder automates page feeding.
Carbon / impact paper Carbon paper placed between plain sheets transfers the print head's mark to all layers simultaneously. Used for making multiple copies in one pass.
Tractor-fed paper Removable perforated side strips with holes engage studded rollers at each end of the platen. Reduces skewing and slippage; ideal for multi-part forms. Ensure holes engage sprockets and set the lever to tractor feed mode.

Components and Maintenance

  • Ribbon: Modern printers use cartridge ribbons that slot over or around the print head carriage, forming a continuous loop. Older models used two-spool ribbons with a sensor and reversing mechanism. Replace the ribbon holder and contents as an integrated component when print quality deteriorates. Some printers use reusable cartridges.
  • Print head: Replace per manufacturer instructions. Caution: the print head can become very hot during use.
