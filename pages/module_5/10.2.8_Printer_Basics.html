10.2.8 Printer Basics

Clearing a Paper Jam

  1. Note the error message; it will identify which tray is affected.
  2. Pull out the indicated paper tray to attempt removal from the front.
  3. If the paper is inaccessible from the front, open the back panel. The jammed paper is typically reachable from this side.
  4. Gently remove the jammed paper, close the back panel, and reinsert the tray.

Replacing a Toner Cartridge

  1. When the printer reports low toner, press the cabinet release button and flip the front panel door down to access the cartridge tray.
  2. Pull out the cartridge tray and remove the depleted cartridge. Send it for recycling.
  3. Remove the new cartridge from its box and shake it gently side to side to distribute the toner evenly.
  4. Do not touch the bottom of the new cartridge. Keep it out of direct light.
  5. Insert the new cartridge into the tray and close the printer.

Replacing a Fuser

The fuser bonds toner to paper using heat and pressure. A failing fuser causes print quality problems.

  1. Power off the printer using the power switch.
  2. Allow the printer to cool for at least 10 minutes; fusers get very hot.
  3. Unplug the power cable to ensure no electricity is running through the machine.
  4. Open the back panel and pull out the fuser. Inspect the drive gear at the same time to determine if it also needs replacing.
  5. Remove the new fuser from its packaging, install it, and reassemble the printer.
