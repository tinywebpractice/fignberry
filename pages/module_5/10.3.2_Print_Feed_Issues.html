10.3.2 Print Feed Issues

Overview

If connectivity with the printer is confirmed but multiple jobs still do not print, the issue is likely mechanical. Print feed problems fall into three categories: paper jams, paper feed/misfeed issues, and grinding noises.

Paper Jam Issues

A paper jam occurs when a sheet becomes lodged in the paper path. The printer control panel typically identifies the jam location.

  • Gain proper access to the stuck sheet without force to avoid further damage.
  • Most sheets can be pulled free by hand.
  • If paper is stuck in the fuser unit of a laser printer, use the release levers. Forcibly pulling paper through the fuser can damage rollers and leave debris.
  • In inkjet printers, the jam location is usually visible. If the sheet will not come out easily, consult the instruction manual to release components blocking removal.

Frequent/recurring jams are caused by unsuitable media, creased or improperly loaded sheets, or faulty rollers. Identify whether the jam occurs in the same location each time and perform targeted preventive maintenance.

Jams within the drum assembly before fusing (laser): A faulty static eliminator may be responsible. This component removes the high static charge from paper as it exits the transfer unit. If it fails, paper may stick to the drum or curl as it enters the fuser.

Always check media and pickup rollers first. If both are in good condition and jams persist, investigate other specific components.

Paper Feed Issues

Symptoms include paper not feeding into the printer, or multipage misfeed where feed rollers pull two or more sheets at once instead of one.

  • Verify paper size and weight are compatible with the selected tray. Confirm the media is loaded correctly with guides set properly.
  • Ensure paper is not creased, damp, or dirty.
  • Fan the edge of the paper stack with a thumb before loading to separate sheets. Avoid excessive fanning, which can generate static and cause sheets to cling together.
  • If media is not the issue, replace the pickup rollers. On laser printers, pickup rollers are part of the maintenance kit.

Grinding Noise Issues

Printer Type Likely Cause Resolution
Laser Toner cartridge, fuser, or gears/rollers Identify noise source, reseat all components, and check the paper path for jams or debris. Replace the toner cartridge, maintenance kit, or both if the issue persists.
Inkjet Fault in the carriage mechanism Consult vendor documentation to re-engage the clutch mechanism with the gear that drives the cartridge carriage.

Common Jam Causes

Unsuitable Media
Paper or labels incompatible with the printer type or tray specification.
Creased Paper
Damaged or folded sheets that cannot travel the paper path smoothly.
Faulty Roller
Worn or dirty pickup/feed rollers that fail to grip or advance paper correctly.
Faulty Static Eliminator
Laser printer component that discharges paper after the transfer unit. Failure causes paper to stick to the drum or curl at the fuser entrance.
