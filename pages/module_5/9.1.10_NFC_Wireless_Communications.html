Near-Field Communication (NFC)

Range
Standard: 10 cm (~4 in); up to 20 cm (~9 in) on certain devices
Data Rate
Very low; suited for small transactions, not large data transfers
Primary Uses
Contactless payments via point-of-sale (PoS) machines; pairing Bluetooth devices by tapping

NFC Mobile Payments

  • User enters credit card info into a wallet app; the app transmits a one-time token (not the original card number) to the PoS reader
  • The token is interpreted by the card merchant and linked to the customer account
  • Device must typically be unlocked to initiate transactions over a certain amount
  • NFC can be enabled or disabled via Settings on Android
  • Some PoS readers only support specific wallet apps
Wallet App Platform
Apple Pay iOS
Google Pay (formerly Android Pay) Android
Samsung Pay Android (Samsung devices)

Other NFC Uses

NFC can initiate other connection types. For example, tapping an NFC-enabled smartphone to an NFC-enabled Bluetooth headset automatically negotiates a Bluetooth pairing without manual configuration.
