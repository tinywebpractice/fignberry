9.1.2 Mobile Device Wired Connection Methods

Input Devices

Device Description
Touchpad Embedded panel on a laptop for pointer control. Supports multitouch and gestures. Configured via Windows Settings app (sensitivity, tap events, gestures).
Trackpad Synonymous with touchpad, but often refers to a larger-format peripheral device.
Drawing pad / Graphics tablet Large-format touch peripheral used for sketching and digital painting. Requires calibration and gesture configuration via OS settings or device app.
Track point Pressure-sensitive nub embedded in a laptop keyboard. Controls mouse cursor; harder pressure = faster cursor movement.

Stylus / Touch Pen

  • Used with drawing pads and some touchscreens for more precise control than fingers.
  • Supports handwriting and drawing; referred to as natural input.
  • Available in sizes from small smartphone styluses to full-size tablet/graphics pad pens.
  • Drawing pad styluses have removable/changeable nibs for different pen/brush types in digital art apps.

Audio / Video Accessories

Component Details
Microphone Records audio and supports voice calling. On laptops, exposed via a small hole in the top bezel next to the camera lens.
Speakers Audio output. On smartphones/tablets, typically positioned on the bottom edge.
Camera / Webcam Used for video recording, web conferencing, and still photos. Smartphones/tablets have both front-facing and rear-facing lenses.
Headset / Earbuds Provides speaker and microphone in one unit. Wired: 3.5 mm audio jack, USB, or Lightning connector (adapter cable if no jack). Wireless: Bluetooth.
