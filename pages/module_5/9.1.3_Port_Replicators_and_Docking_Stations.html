Port Replicators and Docking Stations

Mobile devices often lack sufficient connection options for desk use. Port replicators and docking stations expand connectivity so a mobile device can function like a desktop PC.

Device Connection Functionality Use Case
Port Replicator Proprietary connector (back/underside) or USB Adds ports for keyboard, monitor, mouse, network. No additional functionality beyond port expansion. Basic desk setup for laptops
Docking Station Proprietary connector or USB Sophisticated port replicator; may support add-in cards, drives via media bay, and full-size expansion cards. Full desktop replacement for laptops
Smartphone/Tablet Dock USB or Lightning port Connects mobile device to monitor, external speakers, and keyboard/mouse. Desktop-like use of a smartphone or tablet
