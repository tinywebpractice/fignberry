Owner Account Setup

Most smartphones are single-user devices. The owner account is configured on first use or after re-initialization and is used to manage the app store and device services. Credentials include a unique ID (email address) plus authentication such as pattern lock, fingerprint, or face ID. Accounts can be linked to a phone number or alternate email for verification and recovery.

Platform Required Account
iOS Apple ID
Android Google Account or vendor account (e.g., Samsung Account)

Cloud Service Accounts

Additional accounts enable app settings and data to sync across multiple devices (e.g., contacts accessible on both phone and laptop).

Microsoft 365
Microsoft digital identity (@outlook.com by default, or third-party address). Provides access to Office productivity suite and OneDrive cloud storage.
Google Workspace
Google Account (@gmail.com). Provides free access to Google Workspace productivity apps and free storage tier on Google Drive.
iCloud
Apple ID (@icloud.com). Provides free access to Apple productivity apps and free storage tier on iCloud.

Sub-accounts and App Accounts

  • Device owners can add sub-accounts for services outside their primary account (e.g., a corporate email account)
  • Individual apps can also set up their own accounts (e.g., Facebook, LinkedIn)
  • Account settings control which features sync data to the device and allow adding or deleting accounts
