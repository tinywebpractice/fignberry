Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM)

EMM is a class of management software that applies security policies to mobile devices and apps in the enterprise. The challenge of identifying and managing all devices on a network is called visibility. One common deployment model is bring your own device (BYOD), where employees use personally owned devices to access corporate accounts, apps, and data.

Function Acronym Scope Key Capabilities
Mobile Device Management MDM Device level Authentication policies, policy enforcement, feature use (camera, microphone), connectivity settings, device resets, remote wipes
Mobile Application Management MAM App level Controls which apps can process corporate data; prevents data transfer to personal apps; configures enterprise-managed container/workspace; enforces data loss prevention (DLP) controls

Enterprise Workspace Mode

  • When enrolled in MAM, a device can be locked to only authorized corporate applications
  • Corporate apps (email, calendar, cloud storage, contacts) store data separately from personal apps
  • Messages and attachments may be subject to DLP controls to prevent unauthorized forwarding of confidential or privacy-sensitive data

Private App Distribution

Apple Business Manager
Apple's enterprise program for private app distribution; allows a MAM suite to push apps directly to enrolled devices.
Managed Google Play
Google Play's private channel option; allows a MAM suite to push approved apps to enrolled Android devices.

EMM solution providers include Omnissa ONE, Microsoft Intune, Broadcom, and Citrix Endpoint Management.
