9.3.6 Lesson Review

Question 1

When disassembling a laptop, what is a recommended practice to ensure screws are not lost and can be reassembled correctly?

  • Use screws of the same size for all parts of the laptop to simplify reassembly.
  • Take a photo of the underside of the laptop and tape screws to the relevant points in the photo. ✓
  • Use a magnetic screwdriver to hold all screws together in one place.
  • Place all screws in a single container to avoid losing them.

Photographing the underside and taping each screw to its location in the photo ensures you know which screw goes where during reassembly. Laptops use different sized screws throughout, so a single container is not sufficient. Magnetic screwdrivers risk ESD damage and do not track individual screw locations.

Question 2

A technician plans a memory upgrade for a fleet of computers for sales users. When evaluating the various types of memory modules, what module types are commonly used for ultraportable laptops? (Select two.)

  • Mini-DIMM ✓
  • Micro-DIMM ✓
  • DIMM
  • SODIMM

Mini-DIMM and Micro-DIMM are smaller than SODIMM and are used in ultraportable laptop models. SODIMM is the standard laptop form factor but is not specific to ultraportables. DIMM is a full-size desktop module and does not fit in any laptop.

Question 3

A Windows-based laptop has an internal battery that requires tools to access and remove. What steps must a repair technician take prior to opening the laptop to install a replacement? (Select two.)

  • Discharge the battery.
  • Power the laptop off. ✓
  • Charge the battery.
  • Disconnect the AC power. ✓

All power must be removed before opening a laptop for internal work. Powering off and disconnecting AC are the required safe work steps. Discharging or charging the battery beforehand is unnecessary and potentially harmful to Li-ion cells.

Question 4

A user reports that their laptop battery no longer holds a charge for more than 30 minutes, even after being fully charged. Based on recommended practices, what would be the best course of action?

  • Replace the battery with a new one. ✓
  • Increase the charging frequency to keep the battery at 100% charge.
  • Fully drain the battery and recharge it to restore its capacity.
  • Use the laptop only while connected to the AC adapter.

A battery that degrades to under 30 minutes of runtime has reached end of life and should be replaced. Keeping a Li-ion battery at 100% charge accelerates degradation. Deep discharge cycles do not restore Li-ion capacity and can cause harm. Running only on AC power is a workaround, not a fix.

Question 5

What is the first step you should take before replacing a laptop keyboard or security component?

  • Power on the laptop to test the keyboard functionality.
  • Disconnect the ribbon cable connecting the keyboard to the motherboard.
  • Unscrew the keyboard from the laptop chassis.
  • Remove the laptop's battery and disconnect it from AC power. ✓

Removing all power is always the first step before any internal component work. Disconnecting the ribbon cable and unscrewing the keyboard are disassembly steps that come after power is removed. Testing functionality is a diagnostic step, not part of the replacement process.

Question 6

When upgrading or replacing a laptop's disk drive, which of the following is the MOST critical step to ensure compatibility and proper functionality?

  • Backup all data on the original drive before starting the replacement process.
  • Use a screwdriver of the correct size to avoid stripping screws during disassembly.
  • Place the removed drive in an antistatic bag to prevent damage from static electricity.
  • Ensure the replacement drive uses the same interface type as the original drive (e.g., SATA or NVMe). ✓

Interface compatibility (SATA, mSATA, M.2/NVMe) determines whether the replacement drive will physically connect and function. Backing up data is essential but is a data safety step, not a compatibility one. Correct screwdriver size and antistatic handling are good practices but do not affect drive compatibility.

Question 7

As a technician, you have been tasked with increasing the RAM memory on a laptop by adding a SO-DIMM module to an empty memory slot. Which of the following processes seats the module in the slot?

  • Align the notch on the long side of the memory module with the rise in the memory slot. Insert the module pins into the slot at a 45-degree angle. Rotate the module by pressing down until the side hold-down clips snap into place. ✓
  • Align the notch on the long side of the memory module with the rise in the memory slot. Insert the module pins into the slot at a 90-degree angle to the motherboard and press down until the side hold-down clips snap into place.
  • Add the mounting pegs to the side of the memory module. Attach the ribbon cable and press into place until the mounting clips catch the mounting pegs.
  • Slide the memory into the external ExpressCard slot horizontally until the card is even with the laptop case and you hear a click.

SODIMMs insert at a 45-degree angle and are then rotated down until the side catches snap into place. 90-degree insertion is incorrect for SODIMM slots. Ribbon cables and mounting pegs describe keyboard/touchpad connections, not RAM. ExpressCard is an external expansion interface, not a RAM slot.

Question 8

A non-tech-savvy laptop user orders a new hard drive from a large online retailer to install in the laptop. When the drive arrives, the user discovers that it is physically too big to install. What size drive did the user order?

  • 3.5 inch ✓
  • 9.5 mm
  • 7 mm
  • 1.8 inch

Laptop HDDs use the 2.5" or 1.8" form factor. A 3.5" drive is the desktop form factor and is too large to fit in a laptop drive bay. 9.5 mm and 7 mm are z-height measurements for 2.5" drives. 1.8" is a smaller laptop form factor.

Question 9

Why is it important to document the location and orientation of screws and connectors during laptop disassembly?

  • To simplify the reassembly process and ensure all parts are correctly reinstalled. ✓
  • To avoid damaging the laptop's internal components during disassembly.
  • To ensure the laptop's warranty remains valid.
  • To prevent static electricity from damaging sensitive components.

Laptops use different sized screws throughout and ribbon cables have specific orientations. Documenting during disassembly prevents a reassembly puzzle. Damage prevention is addressed by careful technique. Warranty considerations determine whether to perform the work at all. ESD prevention requires antistatic packaging and grounding, not documentation.

Question 10

A technician needs to replace a solid-state drive (SSD) in a user's laptop. The technician requested the purchasing department to order a drive that has higher bus speeds than other SSDs and fits into a PCI Express bus when incorporating the appropriate adapter. Which drive type does the purchasing department order?

  • mSATA
  • M.2 ✓
  • SATA
  • uSATA

M.2 SSDs interface with the PCIe bus, providing much higher speeds than SATA-based drives. mSATA uses the SATA bus and is capped at 6 Gb/s. Standard SATA is also capped at 6 Gb/s. uSATA (micro SATA) is for 1.8" drive bays and is still a SATA interface.

Question 11

A technician is replacing a laptop keyboard and notices that one of the screws securing the keyboard is stripped. After analyzing the situation, what is the BEST course of action to complete the repair without causing further damage?

  • Drill out the stripped screw and proceed with the keyboard replacement.
  • Skip removing the stripped screw and install the new keyboard around it.
  • Use a rubber band between the screwdriver and the screw head to improve grip and remove the screw. ✓
  • Use a larger screwdriver to force the screw out of its position.

A rubber band placed over the stripped screw head fills the damaged notch and provides enough friction to turn the screw. Drilling is a last resort that risks chassis damage. Skipping the screw prevents full removal of the old keyboard and proper seating of the new one. Using a larger screwdriver risks further stripping and chassis damage.

Question 12

A user has configured their mobile device to unlock with facial recognition. Which of the following methods for securing the mobile device is being used?

  • Antivirus application
  • Trusted source
  • Locator application
  • Biometric authentication ✓

Facial recognition uses the camera to scan a unique body feature and is a form of biometric authentication. Antivirus detects malware. Trusted source refers to approved app installation sources. Locator applications are used to track or remotely locate a lost device.

Question 13

A technician is tasked with replacing an internal laptop hard drive. Which of the following hard drive standards is of MOST concern when selecting a replacement that is compatible with the laptop?

  • The hard drive's form factor ✓
  • The hard drive's read and write speeds
  • The hard drive's minimum voltage requirements
  • The hard drive's storage capacity

Form factor (2.5", 1.8", and z-height variants) determines whether the drive physically fits in the laptop chassis and drive bay. Speed, voltage, and capacity are secondary considerations that do not affect physical compatibility.

Question 14

A technician is preparing to disassemble a laptop to replace a damaged component. They have the following options for organizing screws and connectors. Which method is the MOST effective and aligns with best practices in the industry?

  • Place all screws and connectors in a single container and label it with the laptop model.
  • Use a magnetic tray to hold all screws and connectors together during the disassembly process.
  • Take photos during each stage of disassembly and tape screws to the corresponding points in the photos. ✓
  • Group screws and connectors by size and store them in separate labeled bags.

Photographing each disassembly stage and taping screws to their corresponding photo locations is the explicitly recommended best practice. A single container or magnetic tray keeps screws together but provides no location tracking. Grouping by size is better but insufficient since the same size screw may be used in multiple locations.

Question 15

What types of batteries are typically used in laptops?

  • Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH)
  • Lithium-ion (Li-ion) ✓
  • Nickel-Cadmium (NiCd)
  • Alkaline

Li-ion is the standard battery technology in modern laptops due to its high energy density, low self-discharge, and no memory effect. NiMH and NiCd are older technologies no longer used in laptops. Alkaline batteries are disposable and non-rechargeable, unsuitable for laptop use.
