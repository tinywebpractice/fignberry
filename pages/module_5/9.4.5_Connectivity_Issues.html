9.4.5 Connectivity Issues

General Troubleshooting Guidelines

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity issues can be categorized as physical (interference, hardware) or software (configuration). Apply the same approach used for PC wireless troubleshooting.

  • Verify the adapter is enabled. On a laptop, check function key toggles. On a mobile device, check the notification shade for airplane mode or individual radio toggles.
  • If a laptop was recently serviced and wireless stopped working, check that the antenna connector was not dislodged or incorrectly reconnected.
  • If a Bluetooth peripheral (mouse, keyboard) that was working stops, replace its battery first.
  • If connectivity fails after restoring from hibernate or sleep, try cycling power on the device or reconnecting it; also check for updated drivers for the wireless controller.

Intermittent Connectivity

  • Move the two devices closer together.
  • Change device position or how it is held. The radio antenna in a mobile device is built into the case (typically around the screen); certain hand positions can degrade antenna performance.
  • Use a Wi-Fi analyzer to measure signal strength in different locations and identify interference sources.
  • Use Cell Tower Analyzer or GSM Signal Monitor to analyze cellular signals (cellular uses different frequencies than Wi-Fi). Some apps combine both functions.

Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Steps

  • Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled in network/internet settings.
  • Open the wireless network settings and verify the configuration is correct.
  • If everything looks correct but connection still fails, forget the network and reconnect from scratch. This forces re-entry of the password and resets the connection profile.
  • Check network preferences for auto-connect settings and any required certificates for the network.

Bluetooth Troubleshooting Steps

  • Navigate to connected devices settings; verify paired devices are correctly set up.
  • To pair a new device, follow the manufacturer's pairing instructions for that specific device.
  • If a previously paired device will not reconnect, remove it from the paired devices list and re-pair from scratch. This resolves the majority of Bluetooth connectivity issues.
