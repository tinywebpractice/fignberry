9.4.7 Lesson Review

Question 1

A user states that a mobile phone does not hold a very good charge. What might the cause be? (Select three.)

  • An aging battery ✓
  • Unresponsive digitizer
  • Improper battery charging ✓
  • Lack of power management ✓

As batteries age their maximum charge capacity decreases. Improper charging routines (regular full discharge or persistent 100% charge) reduce usable battery life. Failing to use OS/device power management features also contributes. An unresponsive digitizer affects touchscreen input and is unrelated to battery charge capacity.

Question 2

Six months ago, you purchased a new laptop. Since then, you have noticed that the battery life has steadily gone down. When the battery was new, a full charge lasted about 10 hours. Now, you are lucky to get two hours of life before the battery charge level drops below 5 percent. Which of the following is the BEST first step when trying to correct this issue?

  • Buy a new battery.
  • Calibrate the battery. ✓
  • Clean the contacts on the battery.
  • Replace the power meter chip on the battery.

Over time the power meter drifts out of sync with actual remaining charge. Calibration resets the meter and is the appropriate first step for a six-month-old battery showing apparent runtime issues. Buying a new battery is premature. Cleaning contacts addresses a device that will not power on. Replacing the power meter chip is not a standard field repair.

Question 3

Joe, a frequent visitor to a branch office, attempts to connect his tablet to the office wireless network but is unable to connect to the internet, even though he has verified that the SSID and password are correct. Joe attempts to connect to a coworker's hotspot but is still unable to connect to the internet. Which of the following is the MOST likely cause of this issue?

  • MAC filtering has been enabled on the branch office wireless access point.
  • The connection between the branch office router and the internet service provider is down.
  • Airplane Mode has been set on his tablet. ✓
  • Joe's tablet has been configured with full device encryption.

Joe fails to connect to both the office network and a separate coworker's hotspot despite correct credentials, pointing to a device-level problem. Airplane mode disables all radios, preventing any wireless connection. MAC filtering and ISP outages would not affect both networks. Encryption does not prevent wireless connectivity.

Question 4

Which of the following is a sign of a potential malware issue on a mobile device?

  • Physical damage to the charging port
  • Unusually high battery usage ✓
  • Device overheating due to direct sunlight exposure
  • Swollen battery causing device deformation

Malware and rogue apps run in the background causing excessive power drain -- a direct malware indicator. Physical port damage is from improper connector use. Sunlight-caused overheating is environmental. Swollen batteries result from charging circuit failure or liquid damage, not malware.

Question 5

A warehouse user reports an issue with a laptop to the IT helpdesk. The issue is that the laptop becomes very hot after only a few minutes of use, whether it is plugged in or not. The user states that laptop use is under the usual circumstances. What does IT conclude the likely problem to be?

  • Battery discharging
  • Faulty AC adapter
  • Dust and debris ✓
  • Room temperature

Dust trapped in vents acts as an insulator and prevents proper cooling. A warehouse environment is prone to dust accumulation. The laptop overheats under normal use regardless of power source, indicating a persistent thermal problem. A discharging battery does not cause overheating. The AC adapter is ruled out since the problem occurs on battery power too. Room temperature alone does not cause rapid overheating under normal circumstances.

Question 6

A user notices that a smartphone is not functioning properly as internal diagnostics show a majority of the RAM being used continuously with certain applications. What symptom should a technician look for?

  • Power drain
  • Resource usage ✓
  • Data usage
  • Malware activity

The diagnostic data directly shows RAM being consumed by specific apps. The observable symptom to investigate is resource usage. Power drain may be a secondary effect. Data usage is a network traffic indicator unrelated to RAM consumption. Malware is a possible cause but is not the symptom itself.

Question 7

A traveling user looks to purchase a smartphone with a high tolerance for differing environments. This includes indoor and outdoor use. What ingress protection (IP) rating should the user look for when wanting to protect the device from solids as best as possible?

  • IP58
  • IP67 ✓
  • IP57
  • IPX8

The first digit of an IP rating covers solid protection: 6 = dust-proof (maximum), 5 = dust-protected (not fully dust-proof). IP67 has a solid rating of 6, the highest among the options. IP58 and IP57 both have a solid rating of 5. IPX8 has no solid protection rating at all (X = unrated).

Question 8

After restoring the operating system on a mobile device, the touchscreen operates unpredictably. This happens in both the default orientation and when the screen is rotated. Which of the following BEST helps you correct the problem?

  • App Store
  • Wi-Fi calling
  • Virtual Assistant
  • Screen calibration ✓

Unpredictable touch input after an OS restore is a classic calibration issue; the restore may not have retained previous touch calibration settings. Windows and some Android versions support recalibration utilities. The App Store, Wi-Fi calling, and Virtual Assistant are unrelated to touchscreen calibration.

Question 9

You are a field technician troubleshooting a laptop that does not power on when disconnected from AC power. The battery is properly seated, but the laptop only remains on for a few seconds before shutting down. You suspect the battery may be the issue. What should you do next?

  • Replace the motherboard immediately.
  • Replace the AC adapter with a higher wattage adapter.
  • Test the laptop with a known good battery. ✓
  • Clean the battery contacts with water and a soft cloth.

When a properly seated battery causes shutdown within seconds, the next diagnostic step is to test with a known good battery. If that works, the original battery is confirmed bad. If it still fails, the issue is in the motherboard's power circuitry. Replacing the motherboard before confirming the battery is premature. The AC adapter is irrelevant when the problem occurs on battery power. Battery contacts should be cleaned with isopropyl alcohol (90% or higher), not water.

Question 10

Which of the following is a common symptom of connectivity issues in mobile devices?

  • Overheating of the device
  • Difficulty connecting to Wi-Fi or Bluetooth ✓
  • Swollen battery
  • Physical damage to the screen

Connectivity issues manifest as difficulty connecting to Wi-Fi or Bluetooth networks and peripherals. Overheating is a thermal/hardware failure symptom. A swollen battery is a power/battery symptom. Physical screen damage is a display symptom. Each of these belongs to a different troubleshooting category.
