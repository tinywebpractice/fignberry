11.1.10 Knowledge Base

Knowledge Base Overview

A knowledge base is a self-serve central repository for information. IT service organizations use it to house troubleshooting articles, FAQs, checklists, and remedies for past issues. A ticketing system may also have the ability to store knowledge base content directly.

Content in a knowledge base often originates from previous service requests that the IT support team has resolved. Examples include a checklist to log out and restart a system as a first troubleshooting step, or basic printer and copier troubleshooting steps.

Internal vs. External Knowledge Bases

Internal
Organization-specific articles, checklists, and troubleshooting procedures built from past service requests. May be integrated with the ticketing system.
External (OEM/vendor)
Manufacturer-maintained repositories of FAQs, help articles, and support documentation. Built and maintained by the vendor. Examples include Microsoft Learn (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/support/), Dell support, and HP support. Access via the Help or Support section of each OEM website.
