11.1.12 Policy Documentation

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

An AUP defines what a user is permitted to do with a given service or resource. It can apply in multiple contexts, governing employee use of company equipment and services, or enforcing fair use of internet access provided to customers.

Enforcing an AUP protects the organization from security and legal liability arising from misuse of its equipment. Typical AUP provisions include:

  • Prohibition of using equipment to defraud, defame, or obtain illegal material
  • Prohibition of installing unauthorized hardware or software
  • Explicit prohibition of actual or attempted intrusion (snooping)
  • Restriction or prohibition of non-work-related internet use during work hours

Regulatory Compliance Notices

Beyond AUPs, regulatory compliance requirements may need to be implemented as logical controls or on-screen notices. A common example is a splash screen displayed at login to remind users of data handling requirements or other regulated use policies for a workstation or network application.
