11.1.14 Lab: Close a Ticket

This is a CompTIA exercise. The activity involves updating and closing a help desk ticket in Issue Trax after the requested software installation has been completed.

Scenario

IT technician Jamie Anderson installed Audacity on Taylor Quick's system after hours. Ticket #12 (Need Audacity installed) must now be updated with a resolution comment and closed to reflect the completed work.

Steps

  • Open Issue Trax: Start, All apps, Issue Trax. Maximize the window.
  • From the right pane, select ticket #12 Need Audacity installed.
  • In the New Comment field, enter: "Taylor, I installed Audacity on your system last night." Select the + (plus sign) to add the comment.
  • From the top of the ticket, select Closed, then select the left arrow to save the status change.
  • Verify closure: select Closed from the left pane and confirm #12 Need Audacity installed appears in the list.

Key Concepts

Resolution comment
Documents the action taken to resolve the ticket. Provides end-user confirmation of what was done and when, and creates a record for future reference.
Closing a ticket
Moves the ticket from active to closed status. Tickets should only be closed after the resolution has been implemented and documented. Closed tickets remain searchable for reporting and knowledge base purposes.
