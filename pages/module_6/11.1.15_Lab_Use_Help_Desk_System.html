11.1.15 Lab: Use Help Desk System

This is a CompTIA A+ hands-on lab activity completed within the CertMaster Learn simulation environment. The steps below serve as a reference for the IssueTrax ticketing system workflow practiced in this lab.

Lab Objective

Navigate a simulated IT help desk ticketing system (IssueTrax), read and action an assigned ticket, and close the ticket after completing the requested task.

Procedure

Step Action
1 Open IssueTrax: Start > All Apps > Issue Trax. Maximize the window.
2 Select the Ticket training ticket and read the description.
3 Select Floor 1 Overview at the top of the screen.
4 In the Networking Closet, select Hardware. Drag the Mug to Inventory.
5 Return to Floor 1 Overview. Under IT Administration, select ITAdmin.
6 Type a comment in the Comments field indicating the task is complete, then click the Plus icon.
7 At the top of the ticket, set the status to Closed.

Key Concepts

Ticketing System
Software used by help desk teams to track, manage, and resolve IT support requests. IssueTrax is the simulated example used in this lab.
Ticket Lifecycle
A ticket moves through states such as Open, In Progress, and Closed. Closing a ticket requires documenting the resolution in the comments before changing the status.
Asset/Inventory Tracking
IT environments track physical assets (hardware, peripherals, etc.) in an inventory system, often integrated with or alongside the help desk platform.
