11.1.5 Ticket Management

Ticket Management Overview

After a ticket is opened, the troubleshooting process is applied until resolution. The system must track ownership (who is handling the ticket) and status (what has been done) at each stage. Clear written communication is required throughout, including across escalation routes.

Escalation

Escalation occurs when an agent cannot resolve the ticket. Common reasons include:

  • The incident is related to a problem requiring analysis by senior technicians or a third-party/warranty support service.
  • The incident severity needs to be upgraded (minor to major, or major to critical), requiring senior decision-maker involvement.
  • The incident requires sales or marketing involvement to handle service complaints or refund requests.

Support Tier Model

Tier Description
Tier 0 Self-service options: knowledge base (FAQs and common troubleshooting procedures) or help bot. User attempts resolution before filing a ticket.
Tier 1 Initial agent contact for diagnosis and possible incident resolution.
Tier 2: Internal Escalation to senior technicians within the organization.
Tier 2: External Escalation to a third-party support group.
Tier 3 Escalation as a problem to a development/engineering team or to senior managers and decision-makers.
