11.1.7 Support Documentation and Knowledge Base Articles

Inventory and Support Documentation

Each inventory record should be linked to relevant support sources at minimum including the product documentation/setup guide, a deployment checklist, and a secure configuration template.

Cross-referencing the inventory and ticketing systems allows incident and problem statistics to be associated with specific assets for analysis and reporting, and allows agents to view the full ticket history for any given asset.

Knowledge Base

A knowledge base is a repository of articles covering FAQs, common troubleshooting scenarios, and significant support examples. It serves two purposes: enabling end-user self-service (Tier 0 support) and assisting technicians during diagnosis.

  • Internal knowledge base: Tagged to inventory records for self-service support and technician reference. Articles document organization-specific procedures and known issues.
  • External knowledge base: Links to external articles, blog posts, and forum posts stored in an asset notes field. Always evaluate the author's credentials and verifiable expertise before relying on external content to determine legitimacy.
