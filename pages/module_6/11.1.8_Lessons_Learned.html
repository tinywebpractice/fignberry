11.1.8 Lessons Learned

Lessons Learned / After-Action Reports

For critical and major incidents, a formal lessons learned report (also called an after-action report or AAR) may be developed. This report solicits input from users/customers, technicians, managers, and stakeholders with a business or ownership interest in the issue.

Purpose
Identify underlying causes of the incident and recommend remediation steps or preventive measures to reduce the risk of recurrence.
Outputs
Incident reports and support tickets can be used to create new SOPs or revise existing SOPs and policies. Reports are also filed into the organization's knowledge base for record-keeping and future reference during similar incidents.
