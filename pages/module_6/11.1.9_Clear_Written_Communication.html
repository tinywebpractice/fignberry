11.1.9 Clear Written Communication

Ticket Text Fields

Free-form text fields capture descriptive information across the ticket life cycle:

Issue description
Records the initial request with any detail collectible at the time of submission.
Progress notes
Records what diagnostic tools and processes have discovered, including identification and confirmation of a probable cause.
Problem resolution
Sets out the plan of action and documents successful implementation, testing, and confirmed full system functionality. Should also record end-user or customer acceptance that the ticket can be closed.

Written Communication Standards

At any point in the ticket life cycle, other agents, technicians, or managers may need to make decisions or continue troubleshooting using only the ticket record. Tickets may also be forwarded to customers as a record of work performed. All text fields must therefore use clear and concise written communication with attention to spelling, grammar, and style.

Clear
Use plain language rather than jargon.
Concise
Use as few words as possible in short sentences. State only the minimum facts and actions required to describe the issue or process.
