11.2.2 Professional Support Delivery

Overview

Respect is a core hallmark of professionalism. It means treating others and their property as you would want to be treated. Professional support delivery is built on four main behaviors: punctuality, minimizing distractions, and handling confidential or private materials appropriately.

Be On Time

  • Arrive on time for every in-person appointment or contact call.
  • If a delay is unavoidable, notify the customer as soon as possible.
  • Be accountable and honest about delays, but frame communication positively.

Examples of positive, direct communication about delays or issues:

  • "I'm sorry I'm late - show me this faulty PC and I'll start work right away."
  • "The printer needs a new fuser and I don't have this type with me. I'll call the office to find out how quickly we can get one."
  • "I haven't seen this problem before, but I've taken notes and will check it out when I get back. I'll call you this afternoon - is that OK?"

Avoid Distractions

  • Do not allow interruptions while working at a customer site unless a genuinely critical incident takes priority.
  • Only take calls from colleagues if the matter is work-related and urgent.
  • Do not take personal calls or texts except in a genuine family emergency.
  • Do not browse websites, play games, or respond to social media posts.
  • When on a telephone call with a customer, always ask permission before placing the call on hold or transferring it.

Handle Confidential and Private Materials Appropriately

Demonstrate respect for the customer's property, including any confidential or private data stored on PCs, smartphones, or in printed documents.

Scenario Required Action
Data files, email apps, contact managers, or signed-in web pages are open Ask the customer to close them before you begin work; do not open them yourself
Printed confidential materials (bank statements, personal letters) are visible Do not look at them; make the customer aware and allow time for them to be put away
Customer equipment and services (PCs, printers, web access, phones) Use only for resolving the ticket; no personal use
Physical workspace during a site visit Keep the area clean and tidy; leave it as you found it
