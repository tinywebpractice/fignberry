11.2.3 Professional Appearance

Overview

Professional presentation encompasses three key areas: appearance and attire, proper language use, and cultural sensitivity.

Appearance and Attire

Dress and grooming must reflect the professionalism of your company. If no uniform is provided, wear clothes appropriate to the environment.

Dress Code Description When Required
Business formal Matching suit in sober colors; minimal accessories or jewelry Initial client meetings
Business casual Smart clothes; excludes jeans, shorts, short skirts, T-shirts, and vests Typical troubleshooting appointments
Dust protection suit / coveralls Worn when maintenance may soil clothing (e.g., running cable through an attic or crawl space) Planned dirty/messy maintenance; must be company-approved

Using Proper Language

  • On arrival, make eye contact, greet the customer, and introduce yourself and your company.
  • When answering the phone, introduce yourself, your department, and offer assistance.
  • Use clear, concise statements. Avoid jargon, abbreviations, and acronyms the customer may not understand.
  • Do not be over-familiar. Avoid slang and any language that could cause offense. Use "Hello" or "Good morning" rather than "Hey!"

Example of avoiding jargon:

Poor (jargon-heavy) Better (plain language)
"Looking at the TFT, can you tell me whether the driver is signed?" "Is a green check mark displayed on the icon?"

Cultural Sensitivity

Cultural sensitivity means being aware of customs and habits of others. Culture is shaped not just by nationality but also by social class, profession, leisure, and other factors.

  • Start on a formal basis (e.g., use professional titles such as "doctor" or "judge") and shift to informal address only if the customer signals comfort with it.
  • Do not make stereotyped assumptions about individuals based on perceived cultural background.
  • When a language barrier exists, use questions, summaries, and restatements to clarify. Consider visual aids or demonstrations instead of verbal explanations.
  • Be aware of personal space preferences, which vary across cultures.
