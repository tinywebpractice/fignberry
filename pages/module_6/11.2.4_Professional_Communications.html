11.2.4 Professional Communications

Overview

Listening carefully to customers provides clues to their technical level, allowing you to pace and adapt your replies accordingly.

Active Listening

Active listening is giving your full attention to the speaker without arguing with, commenting on, or misinterpreting what they have said. The goal is to focus on what is being said rather than on formulating your reply or reacting to background distractions.

  • Reflect phrases the customer uses to show you are engaged.
  • Restate the issue and summarize what the customer said to confirm understanding.
  • Take notes during the conversation to maintain an accurate record.
