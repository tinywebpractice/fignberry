11.2.7 Dealing with Difficult Customers

Four-Step Approach

Step Action Detail
1 Identify early signs of anger Watch for raised voice, speaking too quickly, or interrupting. Respond with a low, calm voice, soothing language, and a focus on positive actions.
2 Do not take complaints personally Any anger directed at you is a symptom of the customer's frustration or anxiety, not a personal attack.
3 Let the customer explain while you actively listen Draw out the facts and use them to build a positive action plan that drives the support case forward.
4 Hang up if necessary Follow organizational policy. If a customer is abusive or threatening, issue a caution first. If abuse continues, end the call or escalate to a manager. Document and explain your reasons.
