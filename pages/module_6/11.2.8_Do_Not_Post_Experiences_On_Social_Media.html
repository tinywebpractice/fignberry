11.2.8 Do Not Post Experiences on Social Media

Key Rules

  • Never disclose customer incidents, cases, or difficult experiences via social media or any public outlet.
  • If you need to vent, do so privately with a colleague in a face-to-face conversation.
  • Always exercise discretion and professionalism when referencing specific incidents you are involved in.
  • Anything posted publicly is very difficult to withdraw and can cause unpredictable reactions from customers and your organization.
