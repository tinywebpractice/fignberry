11.3.3 iOS and Android

Overview

Cell phone and tablet OSes are designed exclusively for touch-screen interfaces. The two dominant platforms are Apple iOS/iPadOS and Android.

Apple iOS and iPadOS

OS Devices Key Characteristics
iOS iPhone; early iPad models Based on macOS; closed-source (only Apple can modify the code); runs exclusively on Apple hardware; provides touchscreen interface and app support; annual version releases; updates are free
iPadOS iPad models 2019 and later Developed from iOS; enhanced multitasking; better Apple Pencil support; versions released alongside iOS updates

Older devices may not support all features or may lose support entirely. Update limitations: support.apple.com

Android

Developer
Open Handset Alliance, driven by Google.
Kernel
Based on Linux; source code is publicly available.
Openness
Open-source; manufacturers can create custom versions for their devices, resulting in a wide variety of Android-powered smartphones and tablets.
Device manufacturers
Acer, Asus, HTC, LG, Motorola, Samsung, Google (Pixel), Xiaomi, OnePlus, Oppo - each may implement unique features or interfaces.
Fragmentation
Google issues regular updates, but individual manufacturers decide when or whether their devices receive them, leading to varied compatibility and support across the ecosystem.
