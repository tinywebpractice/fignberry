11.4.3 Exercise: Incident Lifecycle Mapping

About This Exercise

This is a CompTIA hands-on activity supporting A+ Core 2 exam objectives 4.1 and 4.7. The goal is to identify and visualize the lifecycle of an IT incident, including key steps and decision points. The exercise asks you to produce a flowchart using a tool of your choice (pen and paper, PowerPoint, Lucidchart, Visio, etc.).

Incident Lifecycle Steps

Step Purpose
1. Incident Reporting Incident is identified and reported by a user or monitoring system
2. Ticket Creation Incident is documented in a tracking system to create a formal record
3. Categorization Incident is classified by type or category to route it appropriately
4. Prioritization Priority level is assigned based on impact and urgency
5. Escalation Incident is transferred to a higher support level if it cannot be resolved at the current level
6. Resolution A solution is implemented to resolve the incident
7. Closure Resolution is verified with the user and the ticket is formally closed

Decision Points

Decision Point Yes No
Can the incident be resolved at the current support level? Proceed to Resolution Escalate to higher-level support
Was the resolution successful? Proceed to Closure Return to Escalation or attempt a different resolution
Is the resolution verified by the user? Close the ticket Reopen the ticket and return to Resolution

Flowchart conventions: rectangles represent process steps; diamonds represent decision points; arrows indicate the direction of flow.
