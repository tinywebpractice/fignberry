12.1.6 Privacy Settings

Overview

Privacy and Security Settings govern what usage data Windows is permitted to collect and what device functions are enabled for which apps. Security settings also allow changes to antivirus, browser, and firewall settings.

Privacy Toggle Categories

Data collection
Allows Microsoft to process usage telemetry. Affects speech and input personalization, language settings, general diagnostics, and activity history.
App permissions
Allow or deny app access to devices (location service, camera, microphone) and user data (contacts, calendar items, email, files).

General Privacy Toggles (Windows 11)

Found under Settings > Privacy & security > General:

  • Let apps show personalized ads using my advertising ID
  • Let websites show locally relevant content by accessing my language list
  • Let Windows improve Start and search results by tracking app launches
  • Show suggested content in the Settings app
  • Show notifications in the Settings app
