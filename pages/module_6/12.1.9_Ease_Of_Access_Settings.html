12.1.9 Ease of Access Settings

Overview

Ease of Access (called Accessibility in Windows 11) settings configure input and output options to suit each user's needs. Accessible via Settings or Control Panel. Three main groups: Vision, Hearing, and Interaction.

Settings Groups

Group Options
Vision Text size; visual effects (scroll bars, transparency, animations, notification timeout); mouse pointer and touch (color, size); text cursor (appearance, thickness, indicator); Magnifier (zoom tool, reading, zoom increment); color filters (colorblindness filters, grayscale, inverted); contrast themes (low vision, light sensitivity); Narrator (audio descriptions, voice, verbosity, keyboard, braille)
Hearing Audio (volume, mono sound mixing, visual notifications); Captions/closed-captioning (styles, live captions)
Interaction Keyboard and mouse usability options; speech-controlled input; eye-controlled input
