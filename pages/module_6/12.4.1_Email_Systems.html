12.4.1 Email Systems

Email Deployment Models

  • On-premises: Email server hosted locally by the organization (e.g., Microsoft Exchange).
  • Cloud-based: Email hosted and managed by a third-party provider; accessed via web browser over the internet.

Enterprise Cloud Email Examples

Platform Provider Portal
Outlook Web Microsoft Microsoft 365 portal
Gmail Google Google Workspace

Key Characteristics of Cloud Email

  • Accessible from any web browser with an internet connection.
  • Automatically syncs email and folders across multiple devices.
  • Supports synchronization and collaboration between employees.
  • Personal email accounts (e.g., Gmail, Outlook.com) have been cloud-based since the early internet era.
